QRBee · QR & Barcode Scanner

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 September 2026 · Applies to the QRBee app for Android (package com.yunok.qrbee)

In short:

QRBee has no accounts and no servers of its own. What you scan and generate stays on your device. We do not sell your data. The app may show ads served by Google AdMob, which can process limited device information as described below.

1. Who we are

QRBee (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is published by YunoK (“the developer”). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at [YOUR EMAIL ADDRESS].

2. Information the app handles on your device

The following stays on your device. We do not receive it and it is not uploaded to any server operated by us.

DataWhyWhere it lives
Camera images (live preview) To detect and read QR codes and barcodes. Analysed in real time on your device. Camera frames are not saved and not sent anywhere.
Scan history: the content of each code, its type and format, the time, and whether you marked it as a favourite To show your history and favourites. A local database on your device. You can delete items, or clear the whole history, inside the app.
Images you choose from your gallery To read a code from a picture you select. Processed on your device through the Android photo picker. The app does not get access to your other photos.
Codes you generate and images you save To create a QR code or barcode and, if you choose, save it as an image. Saved only to the location you pick in the system file dialog.
App settings (theme, vibrate on scan) To remember your preferences. Local app storage on your device.

3. Permissions the app uses

  • Camera – only to scan codes. The app works for generating codes and browsing history even if you deny it.
  • Vibrate – to give feedback when a code is scanned (you can turn this off in Settings).
  • Internet / network state – used by the advertising service (see section 4). QRBee itself does not upload your scans.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The app may display banner, native, or full-screen (interstitial) ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data. This is handled by Google under its own policies, not by us.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation on Android under Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK, or certain US states), Google’s consent tools may ask for your choice.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share your scan history with advertisers.

5. What you scan is your responsibility

A QR code or barcode can contain anything: text, a web link, a Wi-Fi password, a contact, a phone number and more. When you tap an action such as Open link, Call, Email, Add contact, Copy, or Share, the content is handed to another app on your device (your browser, dialer, email app, etc.) that has its own privacy practices. QRBee does not verify that a code or the site it points to is safe. Be careful with codes from unknown sources.

6. Sharing of information

We do not transfer your scan history, generated codes, or images to us or to any third party. Content leaves your device only when you deliberately copy it, share it, or open it in another app, and when the advertising service described in section 4 operates.

7. Data retention and your choices

  • Scan history remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • You can remove a single item, or clear all history, at any time from the History screen.
  • You can revoke the camera permission at any time in your Android system settings.
  • If Android’s system backup is enabled on your device, app data may be included in your Google account backup according to your device settings. That backup is managed by Google and your device, not by us.

Because we do not hold your data on our servers, we cannot look up, export, or delete it on your behalf. Everything is under your control on your device.

8. Children’s privacy

QRBee is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us so we can help.

9. Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device’s own security (lock screen, encryption, and so on). No method of storage or electronic transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
